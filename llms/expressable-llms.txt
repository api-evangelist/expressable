# Expressable

> Expressable is an online speech therapy platform offering convenient, personalized speech-language therapy for children and adults. Licensed speech-language pathologists (SLPs) provide one-on-one teletherapy sessions from the comfort of home, treating a wide range of communication disorders including speech delays, stuttering, apraxia, autism-related communication challenges, aphasia, and more.

Expressable serves families and adults across the United States through virtual speech therapy sessions. The platform matches clients with licensed, experienced therapists based on their specific needs, location, and insurance coverage.
